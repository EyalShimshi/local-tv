## Local TV 1.0.0

The first release with the **Android app**, next to the Windows app.

### Android (`local-tv-v1.0.0.apk`, Android 12 or newer)

- Multiview of every live Israeli channel: Kan 11, Channel 12 (Mako's player), 13, Now 14, 16,
  TV10, i24NEWS and the Knesset Channel, plus Knesset committees found automatically on knesset.tv
  every start, every five minutes and on refresh. Off-air channels stay hidden until they go live.
- Mixer: every channel starts muted; **M** and **S** on every tile, several solos at once, mute
  wins over solo; volume up to 200% with a limiter; level meters you can hide.
- Tap a tile for spotlight + solo; long-press to rearrange; three tile sizes (two side by side by
  default); fullscreen with channel switching; picture-in-picture.
- Channels tab: switch on/off, reorder, add a stream (`.m3u8`, embed code or watch.knesset.tv
  link) or a web page, test, edit, remove with undo, reset.
- News tab: 33 Hebrew and English sources (5 on at first), filter by source, pull to refresh, add
  any RSS/Atom feed or a site's home page.
- Hebrew and English, dark theme by default, Material 3.

Install: open this page on your phone and tap the `.apk` file. Later versions install over this
one.

### Windows (`local-tv-windows-v1.0.0.zip`)

Unzip and run `LocalTV.exe` (Windows 10 or 11). See
[DESKTOP.md](https://github.com/EyalShimshi/local-tv/blob/main/DESKTOP.md).

`mapping-v1.0.0.zip` decodes Android crash reports from this version.
